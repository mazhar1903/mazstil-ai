MAZSTIL AI

MAZSTIL AI is the internal content production and publishing tool of MAZSTIL MEDIA, a creative agency in Türkiye. Our team prepares short vertical videos for the brands we work with. When a brand owner connects their TikTok account, a team member can post an approved video to that account using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

How TikTok posting works

  1. The account owner signs in with TikTok and approves the requested permissions.
  2. Our team selects a finished video, previews it and writes the caption.
  3. Privacy level and comment/Duet/Stitch settings are chosen from the options TikTok provides for that account. Nothing is pre-selected.
  4. The video is posted only after a person presses Post to TikTok and confirms.
  5. The account can be disconnected at any time.

The tool is not offered to the public.
