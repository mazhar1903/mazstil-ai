Privacy Policy

MAZSTIL AI · Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Türkçe · Home

Data controller

MAZSTİL MEDİA ("MAZSTIL MEDIA"), Türkiye. Contact for privacy requests: github.com/mazhar1903/mazstil-ai/issues.

What we collect from TikTok

When an account owner connects TikTok through TikTok Login Kit, we receive only what the owner authorizes:

  • user.info.basic: open ID, display name and avatar, used to show which account is connected.
  • user.info.profile (only if granted): username, shown next to the display name.
  • video.publish: permission to upload and post a video that a person has explicitly approved.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, plus their expiry times.
  • Creator settings returned by TikTok before each post (available privacy levels, whether comments/Duet/Stitch are allowed, maximum video length).
  • Post status and the publish ID returned by TikTok.

We do not collect followers, messages, viewing history, contacts or any data beyond the list above.

How we use it

Only to show the connected account, to post the videos the user approves, and to show whether a post succeeded. We do not sell or share this data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train AI models.

Where it is stored and for how long

Tokens are stored on MAZSTIL MEDIA's own computer in Türkiye, encrypted with the operating system's user-bound encryption (Windows DPAPI). They are never shown on screen or written to logs. Tokens are deleted immediately when the account is disconnected; post records are kept for 12 months for client reporting and then deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect at any time inside MAZSTIL AI (this revokes the token at TikTok and deletes it locally) or in the TikTok app under Settings › Security › Manage app permissions.
  • Under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, where applicable, the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data by writing to github.com/mazhar1903/mazstil-ai/issues. We reply within 30 days.

Children

The service is for businesses and is not directed at people under 18.

Changes

We will post any change on this page with a new date.
